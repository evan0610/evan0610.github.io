About Me

I am Tianchun (Evan) Wu, an Electronic Engineering undergraduate at The Chinese University of Hong Kong. My research lies at the intersection of embodied AI, medical robotics, and robot learning, with a particular focus on intelligent robotic endoscopy.

At the Robotics, Perception and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (RPAI Lab), I work on learning-based robotic systems that integrate multimodal perception, language reasoning, and physical control. I currently lead and contribute to research projects spanning autonomous endoscopic navigation, vision-language-action learning, and intelligent endoscopic intervention.

My recent research has increasingly focused on self-evolving embodied intelligence: enabling robotic systems to acquire, evaluate, and improve task skills through interaction and experience rather than remaining limited to a fixed policy or a single predefined task.

My long-term research goal is to develop generalizable and adaptive robotic intelligence for real-world medical environments, particularly systems that can continuously improve while operating safely alongside clinicians. I plan to continue toward research-based postgraduate study in robotics and embodied intelligence.
